a few words about me

23 November, 2017

Heello there! I am Carl Ahlbom, but my friend calls me Calle. I’m a webdesigner/ front-end developer skilled in HTML, SCSS and CMS integration. I create clean, professional and functional websites. I also have a background in graphic design for screen and print - and I don’t mind photography either.

I am based in Helsinki, Finland and I have a bachelors degree in digital multimedia. Before graduating from Arcada - University of Applied Sciences, I studied graphic design/ web & media art in a few other schools and took several different courses on these subjects. For now I work as a freelancer, and earlier I worked at a little digital agency. I have also worked as a teacher in basics of web development.

I’m constantly learning web technologies and other design related topics, currently playing around with Javascript and JQuery. I am skilled in many areas of design and development. But I still have everything to learn...

a few web tools I use

Like most of the sites out there, this site is also HTML5 -the latest version of HTML, which is the contents you see here. The text, images, video and audio etc. are all HTML5 elements here. Hand coded - basic stuff.

Cascading Style Sheets v.3. I really like writing responsive CSS. Without styling the internet would be as crappy as the tv show sex and the city or such. But things gets even more interesting with some nice preprocessors..

Styles on steroids. This CSS preprocessor is the bomb! SASS really makes styling more efficient and fun. Sass reduces repetition of CSS and therefore saves time. Now I just have to master it and become a sassy guru.

Something like a third of the web uses WordPress (I've heard). I really like this publishing platform and I have used it in several projects. I chose not to use WP here though - don't know if I chose wisely - time will tell.

Emmet is awesome! Emmet is developed and optimised for web-developers whose workflow depends on HTML/XML and CSS, but can be used with programming languages too. Faster coding - cleaner syntax.
