a few words about me

23 November, 2017

Heello there! I am Carl Ahlbom, but my friend calls me Calle. I’m a webdesigner/ front-end developer skilled in HTML, SCSS and CMS integration. I create clean, professional and functional websites. I also have a background in graphic design for screen and print - and I don’t mind photography either.

I am based in Helsinki, Finland and I have a bachelors degree in digital multimedia. Before graduating from Arcada - University of Applied Sciences, - University of Applied Sciences, I studied graphic design/ web & media art in a few other schools and took several different courses on these subjects. For now I work as a freelancer, and earlier I worked at a little digital agency. I have also worked as a teacher in basics of web development. I’m constantly learning web technologies and other design related topics, currently playing around with Javascript and JQuery. I am skilled in many areas of design and development. But I still have everything to learn...
